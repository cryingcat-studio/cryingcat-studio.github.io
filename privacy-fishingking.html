Fishing King Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how CryingCat Studio ("we") handles information in the Fishing King app (the "App").

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and the App does not collect or transmit your name, email address, phone number, location or device identifiers.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, caught-fish records, leaderboard records and settings are stored on your device. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Third parties

The App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and shares no information with third parties. Purchases are processed by Google Play and are subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version and its effective date on this page.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: cryingcat.studio@gmail.com
